# PinnacleU — Frontend Developer (Shopify / Web)

## Overview
- **Agency / Client**: PinnacleU (for high-growth D2C health brand, makers of GoPadFree, Shark Tank India featured)
- **Role**: Frontend Developer (Shopify Theme & Web)
- **Location**: Bengaluru
- **Working Days**: 5 Days (Mon–Fri)
- **Experience Required**: 1+ years

## About the Role
Our Client is looking for a Shopify Frontend Developer who lives and breathes theme customization. You'll own the look, feel, and performance of their Shopify storefront, building pixel-perfect pages and storefront experiences. This is a hands-on, high-velocity role for someone who can take a design or a rough idea and turn it into a live, conversion-friendly page the same week.

## What You'll Do
- Customize and maintain our Shopify theme using Liquid, HTML, CSS/SCSS, and JavaScript.
- Build and launch landing pages, product pages, collection pages, and campaign pages, often on tight timelines.
- Translate designs (Figma) into responsive, pixel-perfect, cross-browser-compatible pages.
- Rapidly iterate on the storefront to support marketing pushes, sales, product launches, and A/B tests.
- Optimize page speed and Core Web Vitals to improve load times and conversion.
- Configure and customize Shopify sections, blocks, and theme settings so non-technical teammates can update content easily.
- Integrate and configure Shopify apps (reviews, upsells, subscriptions, page builders, analytics).
- Ensure the storefront is accessible, mobile-first, and consistent with our brand.
- Debug frontend and theme issues quickly and keep the store running smoothly.

## Required Qualifications
- 1+ years of hands-on experience with Shopify theme development and customization.
- Strong proficiency in Shopify Liquid.
- Excellent command of HTML5, CSS3/SCSS, and modern JavaScript (ES6+).
- Solid understanding of responsive, mobile-first design and cross-browser compatibility.
- Experience building custom, reusable Shopify sections and blocks with theme settings.
- Ability to translate Figma / design files into accurate, polished pages.
- Comfort working in a fast-paced, rapid-turnaround environment with shifting priorities.
- Familiarity with Git and version-controlled theme workflows.
- Strong attention to detail and an eye for design and UX.

## Nice to Have
- Experience with Shopify Online Store 2.0, metafields, and metaobjects.
- Basic JavaScript framework experience (React) or comfort with the Storefront/Ajax APIs.
- Knowledge of SEO and conversion rate optimization (CRO) best practices.
- Experience with D2C storefronts and high-traffic sale events.
