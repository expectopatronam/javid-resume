# Lumel — Junior React Developer (0–2 Years)

## Overview
- **Company**: Lumel ([lumel.com](http://lumel.com))
- **Role**: Junior React Developer (0–2 Years)
- **Location**: Chennai (Work from Office)
- **Listing**: [Lumel on Indeed](http://in.indeed.com/cmp/Lumel)

## About the Role
We are looking for passionate and motivated Junior React Developers with 0–2 years of experience to join our dynamic team at Lumel. You will be working on cutting-edge web applications, focusing on interactive matrix, grid, and chart visualizations that deliver high performance and exceptional user experiences.

## Key Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain React-based web applications with a focus on performance and scalability.
- Implement and customize data visualizations (matrices, grids, charts, etc.).
- Collaborate with designers, product managers, and senior developers to deliver intuitive user interfaces.
- Write clean, maintainable, and reusable code.
- Debug, optimize, and enhance existing features.

## Required Skills
- Strong understanding of JavaScript (ES6+), React.js, HTML5, CSS3.
- Familiarity with state management libraries (Redux, Zustand, or Context API).
- Exposure to or knowledge of matrix, grid, or chart libraries (e.g., AG Grid, Recharts, D3.js, Highcharts).
- Good understanding of REST APIs and integration with frontend apps.
- Problem-solving mindset with attention to detail.

## Nice to Have
- Knowledge of TypeScript.
- Understanding of data visualization concepts.
- Familiarity with version control (Git/GitHub).

## Who Should Apply
- Freshers with internship/project experience in React.
- Developers with up to 2 years of professional experience looking to grow in a fast-paced environment.
