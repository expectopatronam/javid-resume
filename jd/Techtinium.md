Techtinium Technologies is looking for a talented senior engineer who is passionate about solving business problems through innovation and strong engineering practices. You will drive best practices across the software development lifecycle, mentor junior engineers, and take ownership of building scalable and performant mobile applications.

You'll work in a collaborative, thought-provoking and positive environment — one that values diversity of thought and creative solutions that are in the best interest of our global customers.

While deep hands-on experience with Flutter, Swift, or Kotlin is expected, we care most about hiring people who can master new technologies quickly and bring sound engineering judgment to the table.

Responsibilities

    Architect and build elegant, high-performance mobile applications using modern technologies
    Drive simple, scalable architecture and champion the use of reusable components across projects
    Lead the design, development, and maintenance of new and existing UI components

    Partner closely with cross-functional teams (product, design, QA) to support the entire application lifecycle

    Strong foundation in computer science with deep competencies in Data Structures, Algorithms, and Software Design
    Demonstrated expertise in mobile application architecture, with a strong portfolio of published apps on the App Store or Play Store
    Experience mentoring engineers and leading technical initiatives within a team
    Excellent problem-solving skills with an analytical, detail-oriented mindset
    Ability to work independently, take ownership of features end-to-end, and collaborate effectively across teams
