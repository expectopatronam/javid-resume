Job Description

As an SDE II Full-Stack Mobile Engineer at Avaz (www. Avazapp.com ), you'll lead the development of next-generation assistive technology products that transform the lives of children with disabilities worldwide. Your expertise across mobile, cloud, and AI technologies will drive innovation while creating meaningful social impact. Responsibilities: - Architect and develop sophisticated features for our Flutter-based mobile applications and Web applications. - Lead technical implementation from concept to production deployment. - Design and implement robust automation solutions for development, testing, and deployment. - Proactively research and integrate Gen AI solutions to advance our assistive technology offerings. - Mentor junior team members and contribute to engineering best practices. - Collaborate closely with product management to lead releases. - Optimise application performance and ensure scalability. Requirements: - 4-6 years of professional software development experience. - Advanced proficiency in Flutter development and Dart programming. - Strong experience with Cloud development(Next.js ). - Proven ability to design and implement automation solutions. - Demonstrated expertise in applying Gen AI technologies to solve real-world problems. - Full-stack capabilities with strong problem-solving skills. - Experience with system architecture and technical decision-making. - History of leading successful product releases. - Excellent communication skills and ability to lead technical discussions. - Passion for creating technology with a positive social impact. - Experience with native Android (Kotlin/Java) and iOS (Swift) development. Company Summary Avaz (www. Avazapp.com ), we believe communication is a fundamental human right. For nearly two decades, our team has been solving one problem - ensuring that every person has the opportunity to communicate independently, to express themselves authentically and participate meaningfully in education, work and everyday life. Our flagship product, Avaz AAC, is one of India's pioneering Augmentative and Alternative Communication solutions. But we quickly realized that technology alone isn't enough. For communication to truly change lives, families need support, professionals need training, schools need implementation guidance and societies need to embrace inclusive communication. So alongside the product, we built an ecosystem, working closely with speech therapists, educators, governments and parents to make AAC practical, accessible and sustainable. We have impacted over 200,000 people all over the world. We work with schools, governments, clinics and families in Making Every Voice Heard. About the team We are a team of passionate individuals working towards a more accessible and inclusive world where everyone has the opportunity to communicate and be heard. Our team brings together product management, design, engineering, QA, clinical content, customer support and marketing, with empathy at the centre of how we work. We create space for fresh ideas, different perspectives and thoughtful experimentation. Team members are encouraged to question assumptions, explore new approaches and learn directly from the people who use our products. We believe meaningful innovation comes from combining curiosity, open collaboration and a deep understanding of our users. Our company is headquartered in Ohio with an office in India and employees spread across the world.
