About us

Lydon Keats is a small technology company in Tiruppur. We build mobile apps and business software for clients, and we are growing our product team. We are looking for a Flutter developer who enjoys taking an idea all the way from a blank screen to a live app on the Play Store and App Store.

About the role

You will be building and maintaining our Flutter apps for Android and iOS. You will work day to day with our designers, backend developers and product team, and you will own your features from first commit to store release. We use AI coding tools in our daily work, so we want someone who uses them sensibly: you read, test and stand behind every line that ships, whoever or whatever wrote it first.

What you will do

Build new features and keep existing apps fast, stable and easy to maintain.

Connect the app to REST APIs, Firebase, third-party SDKs and push notifications.

Fix bugs, improve performance and keep the code clean and well documented.

Review teammates' code and share what you learn.

Publish and update our apps on Google Play and the App Store.

Keep learning, including the newer Flutter releases and AI-assisted ways of working.

What we are looking for

3 or more years of Flutter experience, with at least 2 apps live on the Play Store or App Store. Please add the store links when you apply.

Strong Dart and Flutter fundamentals: widgets, layouts, animations and the app lifecycle.

Experience with state management such as BLoC, Provider, Riverpod or GetX.

Comfort with REST APIs, JSON and Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics, Storage).

Experience with local storage such as SQLite, Hive, Isar or Shared Preferences.

A good grasp of Clean Architecture or MVVM, and day-to-day use of Git.

Solid debugging and testing habits, and hands-on experience releasing apps to both stores.

Good spoken and written English and Tamil, as our team works in both.

Where you will work

This is an in-person role at our office in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, so you should live in or be ready to relocate to Tamil Nadu.

If this sounds like you, we would love to hear from you.
